# Code of Conduct

SearSuite welcomes constructive participation from everyone. Treat contributors and users with respect in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and specific in feedback.
- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Respect differing experience levels and backgrounds.
- Protect private information and obtain consent before sharing someone else's work or identity.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when this policy is violated.

## Reporting

Use a private reporting channel published by the [SearSuite organization](https://github.com/SearSuite) when one is available. Otherwise, open an issue requesting a private contact channel without including sensitive details. Do not put personal details or security vulnerabilities in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports as availability allows and will limit access to the details.
